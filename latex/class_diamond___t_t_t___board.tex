\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\doxylink{class_board}{Board} class for Diamond Tic-\/\+Tac-\/\+Toe game. 

Implements a 5x5 diamond-\/shaped grid with special winning conditions. A player wins by simultaneously completing\+:
\begin{DoxyEnumerate}
\item A line of three marks
\item A line of four marks
\item The two lines must be in different directions
\item They can share one common mark 
\end{DoxyEnumerate}
